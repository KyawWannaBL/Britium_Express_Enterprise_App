The result must feel like a real production ERP-grade finance workspace, not a prototype.

Important system requirement

The current code already contains:

deliveryman accounting tabs for COD on hand, transferred COD, pending COD, prepaid on hand, and prepaid transferred
finance modules for account balance, account title list, simple transaction, journal voucher entry/list, cash voucher entry/list, general ledger, cash book summary, and financial statement suite
reporting tabs for cash book summary, journal summary, trial balance, income statement, balance sheet, and profit & loss
shared bilingual filters for date, branch, zone, and search
export expectations for CSV, XLSX, and PDF

Preserve these core capabilities, but redesign them into one cohesive Finance and Accounting Portal with proper architecture, workflow, permissions, fraud controls, and real-time monitoring.

Required user roles

Support at least these two main roles:

1. Financial Data Entry Account

This role is for operational accounting staff who create and submit transactions.

Permissions:

create simple transactions
create journal vouchers
create cash vouchers
upload supporting evidence and attachments
save drafts
edit own draft records before approval
search and view permitted records
submit entries for review / approval
view status of submitted entries
view limited operational dashboards relevant to their scope
cannot approve their own entries
cannot post to ledger directly
cannot reverse approved transactions
cannot access full sensitive management analytics unless authorized
2. Financial Manager Account

This role is for reviewers, approvers, controllers, and finance leaders.

Permissions:

view all finance dashboards
review and approve/reject entries
post approved transactions
reverse or adjust under strict controls
manage approval queues
review fraud alerts
monitor branch and zone financial activities
view all reports and statements
export reports
manage thresholds and approval policies
lock periods / close books
access audit trails
supervise reconciliation and exception queues
Role-based access control requirements
Implement real RBAC, not mocked access
Enforce screen-level, module-level, action-level, and data-level permissions
Prevent self-approval
Enforce maker-checker workflow
Support branch-based and zone-based visibility limits
Support approval limits by amount, voucher type, branch, and account category
Support period lock and closed-period restrictions
Log every sensitive action
