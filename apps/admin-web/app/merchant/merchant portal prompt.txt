Create a production-ready Merchant Portal for a logistics, delivery, and COD business.
This portal is strictly for merchant users, not for customers, riders, warehouse staff, customer service agents, finance staff, or admins.

Core goal

Build a modern, elegant, enterprise-grade Merchant Portal that helps merchants manage shipments, pickups, COD, payments, customers, reports, and support workflows in one secure platform.

The portal must feel like a real production SaaS product used by merchants daily for operational control, financial visibility, and business growth.

Important scope

This portal is for merchants who:

create and manage shipment bookings
request pickups
track parcel delivery progress
monitor COD collection and transfer
review historical shipment records
manage customer/receiver addresses
view invoices and statements
handle returns and failed deliveries
access reports and analytics
contact support
manage merchant profile and settings

Do not build internal staff workflows or admin-only operations screens.
